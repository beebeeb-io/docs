Manage your plan from **Settings → Storage & Plan** in the web app (`app.beebeeb.io/billing`).

## Plans

Current plans and prices are at [beebeeb.io/pricing](https://beebeeb.io/pricing) — that page is the source of truth; prices change less often than this doc gets reviewed, but check there first. As of this writing:

| Plan | Storage | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| Starter | 100 GB | Flat price, no storage add-ons |
| Basic | 200 GB | |
| Pro | 1 TB base | Add storage in 1 TB increments, self-serve up to 99 TB total |
| Teams | 5 TB base, 2 seats | Coming soon — not purchasable yet |

Every plan includes the same zero-knowledge encryption — there's no "encryption tier." Starter, Basic, and Pro all come with a 14-day free trial that requires no payment method up front. Beyond 99 TB self-serve, contact support for a custom quote; we don't promise "unlimited" storage on any plan.

## Starting or converting a trial

New signups can start a 14-day trial on Starter, Basic, or Pro without entering payment details. Near the end of the trial you'll be prompted to add a payment method to keep your plan — if you don't, the account reverts to what a trial without conversion leaves you with (your files stay put; you may lose upload headroom until you're back within a plan's limits). Each account gets one trial.

## Changing your plan

Upgrades apply immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep the higher plan's storage until then, and you can cancel a pending downgrade before it lands. If a downgrade would put you over the new plan's storage limit, nothing is deleted automatically: you simply can't upload more until you're back under the limit, and everything already stored stays encrypted and downloadable.

## Storage add-ons (Pro)

On Pro, add extra terabytes beyond the 1 TB base, billed per TB, up to 99 TB self-serve total. Add-ons can be adjusted from the billing page; the price preview shows the prorated cost before you confirm.

## Pausing, cancelling, and reactivating

You can pause a subscription for a fixed number of days, cancel outright (you keep access until the paid period ends, then the account reverts), or reactivate a cancelled-but-not-yet-expired plan. None of these actions touch your encrypted files.

## Billing cycle and invoices

Plans bill monthly or annually — annual billing is discounted relative to paying monthly. Invoices and past transactions are listed on the billing page; export is available from there too.

## If payment fails

An overdue invoice doesn't delete anything. You'll see a clear overdue state on the billing page with a way to pay it directly; resolving it restores normal billing status.

## Cancelling for good

Cancelling a subscription is different from [deleting your account](/reference/faq/#how-do-i-delete-my-account) — cancelling stops billing and eventually reverts your plan; deleting removes the account and all files permanently. See the FAQ for account deletion.
